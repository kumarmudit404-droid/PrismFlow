WHY THIS PART MATTERS
Accuracy is not this project's metric. The claim is about honest confidence, so
the evaluation must measure honesty directly.

IMPLEMENT
  - Accuracy, macro precision / recall / F1
  - ECE (Expected Calibration Error), 15 bins default, configurable
  - MCE (Maximum Calibration Error)
  - Brier score
  - BRIER MURPHY DECOMPOSITION: reliability - resolution + uncertainty.
    Report all three components separately, not only the total.
    Our claim is specifically that PrismFlow improves RELIABILITY, not
    resolution. A single Brier number hides this; the decomposition
    demonstrates it. This one table will do more argumentative work than any
    accuracy figure in the project.
  - AURC (Area Under Risk-Coverage curve)
  - Selective risk at coverage {1.0, 0.9, 0.8, 0.5}
  - Reliability diagram plotting function

UNIFIED PROTOCOL (protocol.py)
  A single function:
      evaluate(model, loader, experiment_id, seeds) -> dict
  It writes, for every experiment in the project:
      results/<experiment_id>/metrics.json
      results/<experiment_id>/metrics.csv
      results/<experiment_id>/reliability_diagram.png
  Every later experiment calls this and nothing else. No experiment script may
  compute its own metrics.

TESTS
  - a perfectly calibrated synthetic predictor yields ECE near 0
  - a deliberately overconfident predictor yields high ECE
  - the three Brier decomposition components reconstruct the total Brier score
  - AURC of a perfect confidence ranking is lower than that of random ranking

STOP. Do not modify models. Do not run new experiments.
