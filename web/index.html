evidence.

Agreement between views should contribute evidence in proportion to the effective independence of those views, not in proportion to how many views agree.

Chapter 1

The problem

When several sources agree, confidence goes up. That is only sound if the sources are independent. If they are copies of one another, agreement is an echo, and confidence built on it is counterfeit.

PrismFlow studies Dependence-Discounted Evidential Fusion under Collusive View Compromise. It tests the hypothesis that discounting fused confidence by estimated inter-view dependence — ENIV, the effective number of independent views — yields fusion robust to colluding or compromised views.

The scope of the claim, stated narrowly

The hypothesis is about REDUNDANT evidence: views that are correlated with each other. It is not a claim about UNRELIABLE evidence, meaning a view that is individually noisy or corrupted but uncorrelated with the others. Part 08 measured that boundary directly: ENIV is flat as view noise rises, because noise makes a view less dependent, not more (docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md L3). Robustness to individually degraded views is not claimed and is not tested for. docs/CONTRACT.md §1, annotation 2026-09-17

How much redundancy does a standard benchmark contain?

Multi-view methods are compared by their view count. Nobody reports how many of those views are actually independent. On Handwritten (UCI Multiple Features), the canonical 6-view benchmark, they are not six.

6 nominal views
3.28 ± 0.07 measured ENIV 5 seeds
0.547 ± 0.011 efficiency ratio 5 seeds

README.md — 5 seeds, mean ± sample std, 800 test samples, test accuracy 0.981 ± 0.003

Six views behave like three and a quarter. That is not estimator noise: permuting each view's features within class — which makes the views independent by construction while leaving sample size, feature dimension and marginals untouched — moves ENIV to 5.51 ± 0.08 of 6. So 2.23 of the 2.72-view shortfall is measured redundancy and roughly half a view is floor.

The redundancy is not spread evenly. Dropping fou or mor costs 0.74 and 0.68 effective views; dropping fac or pix — the two largest views, at 216 and 240 features — costs 0.24 and 0.21, because they are 0.85 dependent on each other. Keeping pix alongside fac buys about a fifth of an independent witness.

Real views carry no ground-truth dependence, so ENIV is APPLIED here and not VALIDATED here. README.md — "What this is not"

Chapter 2

V1 — what the synthetic experiments showed

Parts 01–15 built and attacked the estimator on synthetic data, where the true dependence rho is a parameter that was set. Two results qualify the project's hypothesis, and they point in opposite directions.

Detection — supported

Under a coordinated attack, ENIV falls 3.36 → 2.70 and dependence among the compromised views rises +0.7351 ± 0.0914. The estimator identifies collusion reliably.

Defence — not supported

PrismFlow's attack success rate was higher than naive fusion's in 12 of 12 attacked cells (+0.0267 ± 0.0071 at ε = 1.0, 0/5 seeds better).

docs/CONTRACT.md §1, annotation 2026-09-18 (Part 09) — experiments/chorus/, 5 seeds

As tested, that half of the hypothesis is not supported. … Detection of redundancy and robustness to it are separate capabilities, and only the first is evidenced. docs/CONTRACT.md §1

Two further scope notes from the same experiment. The dependence signal is blind to uncoordinated compromise: independent PGD reached 0.4640 success at ε = 2.0 while rho_bar moved −0.0290 ± 0.0251 and ENIV stayed flat. And the one measured benefit of the discount under attack is that belief in the wrong class is lower when the attack succeeds (−0.0562 ± 0.0069 at ε = 1.0, 5/5 seeds, bars separate).

Chapter 3

V2's challenge — real evidence

V1 ran on synthetic views where dependence was a dial. V2 asks the harder question: can the same machinery work on real, retrieved evidence, gathered from five different kinds of source about the same claim?

Five angles, each with its own connector chain. Their independence is the whole point — a news aggregator and a press-release aggregator are far less independent than their names suggest, which is something Part 21 measures rather than assumes.

Why Reddit is excluded

The Part 19 brief orders the sentiment angle Reddit-primary, StackExchange-secondary. That ordering is not followed, and the reason is not that Reddit is unbuilt:

it is that Reddit cannot work from this network. Measured 2026-09-24: DNS, TCP and TLS are clean to both reddit.com and oauth.reddit.com, and oauth.reddit.com returns 403 "blocked due to a network policy" with any credential. The block is IP-scoped, so REDDIT_CLIENT_ID and REDDIT_CLIENT_SECRET would authenticate and then fail on every fetch. prismflow/v2/angles/sentiment_angle.py

Regulatory is different again: no connector for it has ever been built, so it retrieves nothing on every row. That is a property of this checkout, not a fault of any run — which is why it is drawn dimmed everywhere on this site.

The headline that is not a headline

Calibration could not be computed. Only 1 of 48 rows reached two angles, which is the minimum for dependence, ENIV and fusion to be defined at all. The harness refused to produce metrics rather than compute them on a handful of rows.
